## Next 3–6 months
- **MSR India Research Fellow, 2027 cohort.** The interest form usually opens around spring. You fit the profile exactly: BTech, graduated within 3 years, research experience. It's a strong route to a top PhD program or to industry.
- **GSoC 2027.** Contributor applications are expected in mid-March. Start contributing to Qiskit, PyTorch or OpenCV in December so your proposal is already credible.
- **PhD in India for the July 2027 cycle.** The January windows for IIT Delhi and IISc have closed. Ask your supervisor about direct conversion. PMRF (₹70–80k/month) comes only through institute nomination.
- **Upwork.** Start once you have one polished RAG/agent demo. A narrow profile wins on Upwork, so pitch yourself as "RAG & AI agent engineer".
